01 · IDEA

Light arrives before thunder.

Barq is Arabic for lightning. In a storm you see the flash, then you count, then you hear it. Breaking news works the same way: first a flash, then the facts. The identity is built on that gap. Every story shows when it was first seen and whether it is still developing; every mark is the flash, and the facts are set calmly around it.

02 · THE MARK

The q strikes.

The name ends in q, the one letter with a tail. The tail is struck as a bolt, the width of the stem it replaces, so the word carries its own flash without a lightning icon beside it. Drawn from Funnel Display 800; every straight corner rounded at 22 of 1200 units, the tip at 12, so the cut stays soft.

Keep the bolt the colour of the word. Two colours split the name.
Never slant it for speed. The bolt already moves.
No glow. The flash is in the shape, not an effect.
Clear space: the height of the bowl on every side.
03 · COLOUR

Ion, storm and cloud.

One accent only. Ion is the violet a real strike leaves on the retina; it marks the flash, the developing state and nothing else. Storm carries the wire and the map; white and cool cloud greys carry the reading. No red for urgency, no beige for warmth.

07 · MOTION

Strike, flash, settle.

The loader runs once a session, 1.7 seconds, and skips on a click or Escape: the bolt strikes down into place, the screen flashes twice like real lightning, the letters rise and the bolt cools from plasma to white. On the page, the map's dots land in the order they happened; the wire runs and pauses on hover; developing dots breathe. With reduced motion, all of it is still.
